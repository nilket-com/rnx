# rnx 0084: chained generic inference and iterator inputs

Status: plan. Record 0083 is closed at plan `f060b15` and census `fddd6bf` on `origin/main`. This record implements the first bounded slice selected by that census: 21 direct operations whose item generic is inferred through a container generic, followed by generic iterator inputs whose concrete item type and borrow lifetime can be proved. The pinned Polars 0.55.2 adapter-narrow inventory and 0083 decision table are the input; the release feature set and denominator do not change.

## Measured starting point and limits

The 0082/0083 surface has 2,074 script-available operations, 1,312 value-tested, 1,969 unsupported, 287 refused *proven* family pairs, and 436 *unresolved* function-generic receiver pairs across 34 keys. The 0083 census marks 52 of 265 unavailable function-generic callables as candidates. Twenty-two have chained inference: 21 direct callables on wrapped owners and one `Schema::try_project_with_alias` on a generic owner. The 21 direct callables have no binding, so **21 is the upper bound for new operations from the chained phase**. The `Schema` method remains outside this record; `Schema::from_iter_check_duplicates` and the `hstack` pair remain refused because their item must convert into `(PlSmallStr, Field)`.

The iterator phase has three direct candidates with owned items (`ListBooleanChunkedBuilder::append_iter`, `FileScanIR::gather_after_filter`, `ScanSources::gather`) and five with borrowed string or byte items (`ListBinaryChunkedBuilder::append_trusted_len_iter`, `append_values_iter`, `ListStringChunkedBuilder::append_trusted_len_iter`, `append_values_iter`, `FrozenCategories::new`). These eight are an **additional upper bound**, subject to owner/return reachability and a compiled binding. `Logical::from_str_iter` has four unresolved receiver pairs and contributes bindings only after applicability is proven; `ChunkSet::scatter_single` lacks that family proof and stays out. Thus a full two-phase implementation could add at most 29 direct operations, not 21; the actual yield is measured after emission. Do not count a pair as an operation.

## Binding rules

For the exact bound chains recorded in 0083, select concrete types as a unit: `I: IntoIterator<Item = S>` with `S: AsRef<str>` or `S: Into<PlSmallStr>` becomes `I = Vec<String>, S = String`; `E: AsRef<[IE]>` with `IE: Into<Expr> + Clone` becomes `E = Vec<Expr>, IE = Expr`. `LazyFrame::rename` resolves both string chains independently. Only an exact supported item bound qualifies; `Into<(PlSmallStr, Field)>`, extra unsatisfied bounds, unbound return generics and closure generics retain their current refusals. The generator must choose the owned `String` item, rather than recursively mapping `AsRef<str>` to `&str` and creating a vector of borrows. Rune vectors and their elements are borrowed through the existing support helpers, converted to owned Rust vectors for the call, and remain usable afterwards. Preserve `Option` and other parameter shapes, including `Expr::over_with_options`.

For a bare `Iterator`, `ExactSizeIterator` or `TrustedLen` bound with a concrete, mapped scalar item, convert a Rune vector to `Vec<Item>` and pass `into_iter()` to Polars. For `&str`, `&[u8]` and their `Option` forms, hold an owned `Vec<String>` or `Vec<Vec<u8>>` temporary through the entire Polars call and pass an iterator of references into that temporary. Empty, null and binary values with zero and non-UTF-8 bytes retain their structure. No Rust borrow may escape into a returned Rune value or a stored callback. Apply only to methods whose source contract consumes the iterator synchronously. A `TrustedLen` claim must come from the standard vector iterator or a proven length-preserving adapter, never an unchecked hint. Existing callback audits and engine-thread routing remain in force.

The 0083 compile probe establishes concrete Rust calls, not generated Rune mappings, wrapper reachability or oracle values. Before admitting each iterator candidate, check the owner wrapper, any return wrapper (including `Arc`), mutating receiver behavior, public path, source preconditions and whether its fixture can be constructed. A failure remains a named refusal. In particular, `ChunkedArray::match_chunks` stays refused: its single-chunk and length-sum preconditions are debug-only checks before unchecked slicing in the pinned Polars source. This record does not emit it without adapter-side runtime validation and tests of every failure case.

## Gates

1. **Freeze and audit the target set.** Snapshot the 21 direct chained keys and the 3 + 5 direct iterator keys from `probes/0083/census.json`, with canonical paths, exact generic bounds, source receiver and return types, and current surface reasons. For each iterator key, record `candidate`, `blocked by owner/return`, or `refused` with the specific reason before emission. Audit the four `Logical::from_str_iter` pairs separately through family applicability. Reconcile the 0083 52-candidate census without changing its historical decisions silently.
2. **Generator rule and compilation.** Add narrow, structural generic-chain inference before `unused_generic`, and map the resulting concrete owned parameter types. Add explicit iterator-input lowering for the audited item shapes, hoisting borrowed data through the complete call and respecting routed closures. Synthetic generator controls must cover one and two string chains, `Vec<Expr>` through `AsRef<[IE]>`, optional `E`, bare `Iterator`, `TrustedLen`, borrowed `&str`/`&[u8]` with `Option`, and refusal of tuple-with-`Field`, return-only, callbacks, and `match_chunks`. Regenerate; compile every emitted binding as the second check. A generic-bucket flag alone is insufficient, as 0083's dry run demonstrated.
3. **Behavior and oracle.** Exercise representative frame, lazy, expression and selector chains, including an empty vector, two independent rename vectors, invalid column names, and reuse of the caller's vector after success and error. For iterator inputs test empty/nonempty, null placement, binary `0x00`/`0xff`, and caller-vector reuse; check a mutating builder before and after its call. Generate paired Rune/Rust cases for every emitted binding with a fixture and structural comparator, and report `match`, `both_error`, `both_panic`, unverified and excluded separately. An unavailable fixture does not become a value match. No unapproved mismatch is accepted.
4. **Accounting, cost and evidence.** Run generator self-test, drift/accounting checks, both adapter suites with Cargo fail-fast disabled, and the full oracle. Reconcile the scoreboard by operation, binding and family pair, with the chained and iterator contributions separate; preserve the 287 refused proven-pair baseline except for an explicitly explained mapping change. Retain and hash the closed 0082/0083 release binary before rebuilding, then run three interleaved 60-run cold-launch sets against it under the parity map's +5 ms per-record budget, keeping raw samples. Record exact source contracts, exclusions, case outcomes, hashes and launch measurements in `plans/0084_polars_function_generic_inputs_evidence.md`; update the adapter and generator READMEs for the new input contract.

The remaining function-generic decisions—family-wide instantiation, numeric scalar policy, return-only generics and callbacks needing a fresh 0079 audit—stay in the 0083 table. Arrow bitmap and buffer mappings remain a separate pool.
