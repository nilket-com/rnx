# rnx 0106: tagged, owned snapshots from `ChunkedArray::layout`

Status: plan. Record 0105 closed at plan `1ebdc20` and implementation `a02e099` on `origin/main`.

## Target and source contract

The 0105 scoreboard is 2,135 script-available operations, 1,379 value-tested and 1,908 unsupported. Of 84 refused proven pairs, sixteen belong to `ChunkedArray::layout` (`polars_core:3706`, `polars_core::chunked_array::ChunkedArray::layout`). Target the **fourteen** numeric, Boolean, String, Binary and BinaryOffset owners whose typed arrays and owned cell mappings were proven in 0099–0105. List and Struct retain named nested-value refusals. Fourteen emitted pairs should take the refused proven pool to 70, add one distinct available and value-tested operation, and add fourteen paired oracle cases; the 564 unresolved generic pairs should remain unchanged.

Pinned Polars 0.55.2 `chunked_array/mod.rs:919-947` returns `ChunkedArrayLayout<'_, T>` with four variants: `SingleNoNull(&T::Array)`, `Single(&T::Array)`, `MultiNoNull(&ChunkedArray<T>)`, and `Multi(&ChunkedArray<T>)`. One chunk chooses a `Single` variant based on that array's null count; otherwise Polars chooses a `Multi` variant based on whether every chunk has no nulls. The return borrows the receiver and is not a general script type. Call `layout()` **once** and convert its variant to an owned `(String, Vec<Vec<Option<script value>>>)`: the string is exactly `SingleNoNull`, `Single`, `MultiNoNull` or `Multi`, and the nested vector copies the variant's one array or all of its chunked receiver in forward chunk order. Preserve empty chunks, null positions, values, UTF-8 strings and raw binary bytes. A returned value must outlive the receiver. Do not expose the enum, typed arrays or a borrowed `ChunkedArray` to Rune, and do not infer the tag from the copied data; report the Polars variant actually returned.

Use one inclusive materialize bound for the whole payload: one slot per returned chunk, one per cell including nulls, and one per non-null string or binary payload byte. Preflight the original receiver's chunks using safe typed downcasts and checked sums **before calling `layout()` or allocating any result**. After the call, copy the selected variant's payload under that same total. A `Single` variant must correspond to exactly one preflight chunk and match its cell count; a `Multi` variant must correspond to the receiver's complete preflight chunk sequence. Re-count chunk, cell and byte slots during copy and require the final count and total to match preflight. Refuse a changed shape, wrong typed array, arithmetic overflow, budget excess or value-conversion failure with a typed error and no partial result. Numeric `u64` read-back follows 0093's checked conversion. The tag's small string allocation must also follow the successful preflight. The Rune receiver stays usable after both success and failure. Preserve the existing 0099–0105 snapshot budgets.
