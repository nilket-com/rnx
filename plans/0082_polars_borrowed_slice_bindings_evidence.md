# 0082 borrowed slices: evidence

Plan `23a4abe`. An immutable borrowed slice that a Polars binding returns, iterates or hands a callback is copied into an owned Rune vector under the materialize bound; 50 of the 51 bare-slice pairs are emitted, all six target operations are available and value-tested, and three further slice-returning callables outside the family pool came along under the same rule.

## Gate 1: mapping audit

The 0081 surface's 337 refused proven pairs across 49 keys were frozen; the 51 bare-slice pairs span 12 keys. By shape: 19 direct returns (`cont_slice` `PolarsResult<&[T::Native]>` on 10 numeric families; `Option<&[u8]>` from `first`, `get`, `last` on `BinaryChunked` and `BinaryOffsetChunked` and from `min_binary`, `max_binary` on `BinaryChunked`; `&[Field]` from `struct_fields` on `StructChunked`), 29 iterator items (`data_views` items `&[T::Native]` on 10 families; `downcast_slices` items `&[T::Physical]` on 16 families, including `&[&[u8]]`, `&[&str]`, `&[bool]`, `&[()]` and `&[Box<dyn Array>]`; `iter` and `no_null_iter` items `Option<&[u8]>` and `&[u8]` on the two binary families), and 2 callback inputs (`for_each` with `FnMut(Option<&[u8]>)` on the two binary families). Every slice is borrowed from the receiver for the duration of the call, and every copy runs inside the binding's call, inside `engine::run` when the binding is routed, before the closure returns. Element conversions are the existing scalar ones (`u8` and the other narrow integers as script ints, floats, bool, `&str` as string), the `Field` wrapper, unit, and recursively a slice. Dispositions: 50 `candidate`, emitted and compiled; 1 `still refused`: `downcast_slices` on `ListChunked`, whose element `Box<dyn polars_arrow::array::Array>` is a foreign Arrow object (census: `iterator item: return (slice element: foreign type: …)`). No pair was excluded by the release file.

## Gate 2: emission and ownership

The generator's return mapping admits `Ty::Slice`: the element is mapped as a return, the result type is `Vec<elem>`, the conversion is `support::copy_slice(__r, "<operation>", …)` and the binding becomes fallible. A slice inside `Option`, `Result` or another slice composes through the existing arms; an iterator whose item is a slice materializes under one `support::SliceBudget` guard held for the whole run; a callback input that copies a slice turns the bound's refusal into the callback's typed failure (`CallbackFailure`, naming the operation), so the 0080 guard, budget and unwind path are unchanged; because such a callback copies on every invocation, the binding holds one `SliceBudget` guard around its whole engine-thread call, so every invocation's copies count against one cumulative bound (Codex's review found the per-invocation reset). A mutable slice return falls under the pre-existing `&mut` rule (receiver mutated in place, unit returned), so nothing mutable is exposed or copied.

`support::copy_slice` checks `n > limit || used + n > limit` before allocating, where `used` is the cumulative count of elements copied under the outermost guard (a binding's nested slices, or every item of one iterator), and refuses with `MaterializeLimit` naming the operation and bound; a refused copy returns nothing partial. The production bound is the shared `materialize_limit()` (1,048,576 elements) and `polars::set_materialize_limit` overrides it under test support.

Synthetic emitter controls (`--self-test`, `slice self-test`): `&[i64]`, `&[u8]`, `Option<&[u8]>`, `&[Field]`, `&[&[u8]]` (two copies), an iterator of `&[i64]` (guard plus materialize helper), a callback `FnMut(Option<&[u8]>)` (copy plus typed failure); `&mut [u8]` never copied; `&[Box<dyn Array>]` refused as a foreign type; `&[impl Iterator]` refused as an iterator element. Compilation with all features and without generated bindings is the second check.

Counts, 0081 to 0082: generated operations 2,056 to 2,065 (the six targets plus `GroupsIdx::first` `&[IdxSize]`, `ScanSources::as_paths` `Option<&[PlRefPath]>` and `LiteralValue::extract_binary` `Option<&[u8]>`, which the same rule reached outside the family pool); unsupported 1,978 to 1,969; script bindings 3,851 to 3,904 (+50 family pairs, +3); adapted 327, out of scope 710 and wrappers 270 unchanged; refused proven pairs 337 to 287 across 44 keys.

## Gate 3: behaviour and oracle

`tests/borrowed_slices.rs`: a copied `cont_slice` vector and a `struct_fields` vector are read after their source wrappers dropped; the direct bound is exact (limit 3 copies the 3-element slice, limit 2 refuses with `cont_slice: 3 slice elements with 0 already copied, more than the bound of 2`, and the receiver is usable afterwards); a two-chunk array's `data_views` yields two slices under one cumulative bound (limit 6 copies both, limit 5 refuses the second item with `3 already copied`), while `cont_slice` on it is Polars's own error, not the bound; binary cells copy a zero byte, a non-UTF-8 `0xff` byte and an empty slice through `get`, `iter`, `no_null_iter`, `first` and `max_binary`; a `for_each` callback sees the byte snapshot, its panic reaches the script as `CallbackError`, a bound of 1 fails the callback with the bound's message, and the receiver keeps its length; the two 2-byte cells fit a bound of 3 one at a time but one `for_each` call over both is refused at the second (`2 slice elements with 2 already copied`), while a bound of 4 admits the call. The core binary fixtures now hold `[b"ab", b"\x00\xff", b""]` so every binary oracle case exercises those bytes.

The oracle grew from 2,007 to 2,057 cases; all 50 new cases match Rust. Tally: 1,942 match, 98 both_error, 17 both_panic, 0 row_order_differs, 0 fixture_failed; 2,057 verified. The one status change among old cases is `LazyFrame::unique`, row_order_differs to match, the run-to-run permutation of an unordered case; it is not part of the claimed delta. Value-tested operation paths 1,254 to 1,263: the six targets, `extract_binary` and `GroupsIdx::first` added by this record, plus `unique` by that permutation; `as_paths` compiles but has no fixture. Scoreboard (`probes/0077/scoreboard.py`): available 2,065 to 2,074, value-tested 1,304 to 1,312, unsupported 1,978 to 1,969. The six targets individually: `cont_slice` 10 receivers, `data_views` 10, `downcast_slices` 15 of 16, `min_binary` 1, `max_binary` 1, `struct_fields` 1, all value-tested; the 44 remaining pairs of `first`, `get`, `last`, `iter`, `no_null_iter` and `for_each` extend operations that were already available.
